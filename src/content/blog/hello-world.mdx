# Hello, World!

Welcome to my blog. I'm Irvan, a software engineer based in Jakarta, Indonesia. This is my first post, and I wanted to start by introducing myself and sharing what I plan to write about here.

## Who I Am

I'm a backend-focused software engineer with around 5 years of experience working across different industries — from e-commerce to SaaS. My main tools are **Go** and **Ruby on Rails**, though I enjoy picking up new technologies when the problem demands it.

Currently, I work at Mekari on MekariSign, where I've had the chance to build things from scratch and scale them to handle tens of thousands of users.

## What I'll Write About

This blog won't be a strict technical journal. I'll write about:

- **Engineering lessons** — things I've learned building and scaling systems
- **Personal reflections** — on career growth, habits, and productivity
- **Occasional tech deep-dives** — when I want to explore something in detail

## Why a Blog?

Writing helps me think more clearly. When I try to explain something I *think* I understand, I often discover gaps. Plus, if something I write helps even one person, that's a win.

Thanks for being here. More posts coming soon.
